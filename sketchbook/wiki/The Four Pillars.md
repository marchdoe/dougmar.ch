# The four pillars

Every decision on dougmar.ch gets checked against these four, whether it's a gate, a prompt, a page or a PR. If a change doesn't serve one of them, it needs a reason. Settled with Doug on 2026-10-08.

## 1. Have a point of view

The point of view is mine, and it doesn't change from night to night. It lives in `signals/taste.md`, `signals/voice.md` and the gold standards (the 04-28 terracotta Specimen, the 06-13 fairway Split, the 09-01 trophy-gold scoreboard). Each night the Art Director reads the day's signals through it. The signals give it material. They don't give it opinions.

A night with no position is a failed night, however clean the build.

See [[Taste MOC]] for where the point of view is written down.

## 2. Encode your standards into the machine

Once I've said something twice, it's a standard. A standard that code can measure becomes a [[CONTEXT|gate]] that blocks the night. One that code can't measure goes to a critic, along with the gold standards. Nothing lives only in a conversation or in Claude's memory.

So the response to a repeated complaint is a gate. Fixing that one night doesn't count. The header complaint in `signals/taste.md` (three ratings running in July) is the model case.

See [[Pipeline MOC]] for the gates and critics.

## 3. Refuse to confuse done with good

Every night ships. The machine learns by shipping, and a frozen site teaches it nothing.

Done means the gates passed. Good is my call, and it reaches the machine only as feedback: ratings, taste notes, references. So the two get reported separately everywhere, and a green run never gets described as a good night.

When the nights stop improving, look at the feedback loop before the pipeline. In October 2026 the bottleneck was me. Nobody had rated any of the fifteen nights from 09-23 to 10-07.

See [[Feedback Channels]].

## 4. Unleash creativity and artistry

The standards exist so the designs can take risks. The machine handles anything mechanical, like overflow, contrast, token resolution and the brand lockup, so each night's attention goes to the gesture.

Risk is the default. A safe night that passes is worse than a bold night that misses. The medium has to keep growing, too. Right now the pipeline can only make flat type-and-colour pages ([#505](https://github.com/marchdoe/dougmar.ch/issues/505)). Imagery, motion and new materials come next.

## How the pillars connect

Pillar 1 says what good looks like. Pillar 2 makes the machine hold to it without me. Pillar 3 keeps us honest about whether it's working. Pillar 4 is what the other three are for.
