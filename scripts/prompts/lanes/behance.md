**Lane: Black-Canvas Portfolio Grid**

> Source: portfolio-platform galleries (Behance and similar). General genre characteristics of dark-canvas regular-grid case-study presentation, not a specific copyrighted layout. Use as anchor reference, not copy target.

## Atmosphere
A curator's black gallery wall. Project tiles float on near-black with generous negative space between them, each tile a case-study teaser rather than a single image. More portfolio than mood board.

## Color roles
- bg: #111111, near-black
- bg.tile: #1C1C1C, card surface
- text: #F5F5F5
- text.mid: #9E9E9E
- accent: #1769FF, used only on interactive marks (likes, view counts, active state)
- border: 1px #2A2A2A, visible hairline around tiles

## Typography
- Display: whatever the chassis provides at 600–700, scale ratio 1.333 (tiles support the work, not shout over it)
- Body: 400, 14px, line-height 1.5
- Mono: not used in this lane

## Component cues
- Buttons: pill, outlined (not filled) on dark; blue fill reserved for the single primary CTA
- Cards: tiles carry a visible 1px border, and every one carries a title and a one-line description as well as its image

## Anti-patterns specific to this style
- DO NOT use a white background. This lane is dark-canvas by definition
- DO NOT let a tile lose its border. On near-black the hairline is what separates one case study from the next
- DO NOT omit the title and description. A tile without them is a mood board, not a portfolio
- DO NOT use a red accent. Blue only

## Mobile strategy
Title and description stay legible against the ground rather than overlaid on the image. Overlaid text on a portfolio tile reads as a broken image, not a design choice.
