**Lane: Bold Saturated Stack**

> Source: consumer fintech marketing pages (Cash App and similar). General genre characteristics of chunky, high-saturation color-block sections, not a specific copyrighted layout. Use as anchor reference, not copy target.

## Atmosphere
Loud, confident, chunky, saturated and playful rather than soft and warm. Each section commits fully to one bold color rather than sharing a neutral off-white base.

## Color roles
- bg per section: alternates between full-saturation fields, e.g. #00D632, #B9FF66, #000000. No shared neutral base across sections
- text: #FFFFFF on dark/saturated sections, #000000 on the lightest section
- accent: whichever color is NOT the current section's background, used for the CTA in that section only
- border: none. Color blocking replaces hairlines entirely

## Typography
- Display: whatever the chassis provides at 700–900, scale ratio 1.5, chunky rounded numerals where the chassis supports them
- Body: 500, 16px, line-height 1.5, often centered within a section
- Mono: not used in this lane

## Component cues
- Buttons: large, fully rounded, high-contrast fill (always the opposite color from the section's background), 16–18px, bold weight
- Cards: rare. The saturated color does the containing; a card that does appear is borderless with a solid contrasting fill, not a subtle elevation
- Wordmark: chunky and rounded, set in the bold display cut

## Anti-patterns specific to this style
- DO NOT use a shared off-white base. Each block of the page commits to ONE saturated color, with no neutral showing through
- DO NOT use soft pastel tones. Saturation stays high throughout
- DO NOT use subtle drop-shadow elevation. Flat color blocking only
- DO NOT use rounded-corner containers. The edge of the color is the only container this lane has

## Mobile strategy
The saturated color keeps its extent and its confidence at every width. Reduce padding modestly rather than proportionally. This lane should still feel chunky on a phone, not compressed into a muted list.
