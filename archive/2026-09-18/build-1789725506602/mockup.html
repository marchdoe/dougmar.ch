15th Club
AI · Golf intelligence 2025 Role · Founder, Design + Engineering

Most golf apps are digital scorecards. This one is not.

15th Club reads the round, not the total. It models lie, wind, elevation and the shot patterns a player actually owns, then hands back the one decision that matters on the tee — club, target, and the risk math behind it. Golf plus real intelligence, not a form to fill in after the putt drops.

The proof runs live beside this claim: the Biltmore Championship is on the board right now, two players tied at seven under, the exact kind of moment the product is built to argue about.
