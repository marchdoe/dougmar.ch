## Measured phone density

Text-box area over fold area at the phone (360px wide), light scheme.
A fold is one phone screen counted from the top of the page, and a value is the share of it covered by text.
Under 15% is sparse. This is a measurement and not a fault: a fold given to an image or a field of colour reads as sparse.
