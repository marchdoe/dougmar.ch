===RATIONALE===
The phrase comes from the day's one hard fact that is Doug's kind of fact. Bridgeman won the Biltmore Championship at −26, two ahead of Ben James, and Doug reads a leaderboard the way he reads a box score. The quote lane lost on its own words: "journey" is on the copy gate's list and a revenge proverb is not something he says. That makes the object a figure. −26 takes the poster slot, the phrase becomes its caption, and the five names that finished behind the winner give the page its diagonal, from the score at upper right down to the chasers at lower left.

DM Serif Display is the chassis because the object is numerals. A didone 2 and 6 at 160px is the loudest, most specific thing the catalog can put on a page, and Public Sans keeps the deck and the chase list plain. The type is set small-caps, regular weight, centred, with the phrase stacked one sentence per line. All of that moves off the last week, which ran lower, caps and mixed, left and justified. The work index below steps its row heights down from 152px to 64px, which gives the accelerating rhythm. Fifteen of the seventeen recent shells put a top bar or a rail somewhere; this one keeps its header to a 40px mark beside the wordmark and sends the nav to the footer band.

The palette is a single raspberry flood at 332°, the open gap in the recent hue map, with ivory ink and one fairway-mint spark reserved for under-par figures and the 15th Club row. The 140–180° target sits entirely inside the recent window's forbidden zones, so I took the golf green as the accent instead and put the ground where nothing has been. Drench is a formula the last seven days avoided. Because raspberry and the original green-and-blue mark would vibrate, the mark runs single-color in mint and clears 4.8:1. Grain gives the flood a riso-print surface. The entrance is none: a scoreboard arrives whole, and only the rows below the fold rise on scroll.
