===RATIONALE===
The phrase is a golf score, so a number has to lead. Bridgeman finished the Biltmore Championship at 26 under, two clear of Ben James. That makes the object a figure: "-26" at the largest size the chassis allows, with "Twenty-six under. Two clear." as its caption and the one h1. Doug says the number, then the margin, and a leaderboard of five names proves it in the same fold. The Confucius quote lost on two counts. "Journey" can't reach the page, and a revenge proverb isn't Doug's line.

Big Shoulders Display carries it. It is the Chicago scoreboard face, condensed and heavy, its numerals read as a leaderboard, and it is off the last three chassis. Atkinson answers as the body face for names and deck. The type treatment moves off the recent run: small-caps, centred, stacked one sentence per line. The header moves off the recent left-rail, none and folded-into-hero placements to footer-only with a horizontal 40px mark, so the brand sits top-left of the hero at both rungs.

The palette is azalea drench at 328°, chosen for two reasons. The mandate's 140–180° target is inside the forbidden zone after two days of teal and pine, and the repetition check wants at least 60° from 162°. Azalea is also the flower on Augusta's fairways, so it says golf without the green scorecard cliché. The one fairway-green figure floats on it as the under-par mark and nothing else. Drench is fresh against the recent dark-void, light-ground and split-field run, and grain gives the flooded field a printed-scorecard texture that drifts slowly. The mark goes single-color in ivory, which clears 6.3:1 on the field. The entrance is `none`, since a score that arrives fully formed is the right poster gesture, and the index below rises on scroll.
