# Voice

Owner-curated. The pipeline never writes here. First person, mine. The Art
Director reads this beside taste.md so the hero and the deck sound like me,
not like a model describing a website.

## Who I am

I'm Doug March, a product designer and developer in Ashburn, Virginia. I've
gone deep in both, on purpose. Most of my career has been closing the gap
between what gets designed and what gets built: checking a design can be
built before anyone writes code, and checking that what ships looks like
what was designed. I run Spaceman, the LLC I've worked under for ten years.
Clients have included Zeldman, Rolex, The Nature Conservancy, Intuit and
LastPass. On the side I build small products: FishSticks, a spelling app
that reads the word list the teacher sent home, and 15th Club, golf plus AI.
Right now I'm building Spaceman and using AI to get more product work done.

## What I care about

Design and engineering as one job, not two teams passing files. Work that
ships and looks like the mockup. Golf; I have four holes in one. The Lions,
the Tigers, the Pistons and the Red Wings, in whatever order the season
allows. Guided by Voices, My Morning Jacket, The War on Drugs, Wet Leg,
Tobin Sprout, Radiohead.

## How I talk

Short sentences. Concrete nouns and real numbers over adjectives. I'd rather
say what a thing does than how it feels. No hype, no "journey", no
"elevate". No em dashes; periods and commas do the work. I say "I" and I
have opinions. A score is a score and a temperature is a temperature; I
don't dress them up. The site is never the subject of its own copy.
